ABOUT CARBONWISE

Sustainability starts with understanding.

CarbonWise is a simple demonstration tool for GreenHaul Logistics, a fictional logistics business. It helps users estimate vehicle emissions and see how basic operational data can support informed decisions.

It is designed to make carbon estimation approachable—not to replace verified emissions accounting, official emission factors, or professional sustainability reporting.
