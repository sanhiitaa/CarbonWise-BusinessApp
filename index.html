ABOUT CARBONWISE

Better logistics decisions start with better visibility.

CarbonWise is a sustainability intelligence prototype for GreenHaul Logistics, a fictional Indian freight operator. It brings carbon estimates, transport comparisons and reduction targets into one simple workspace.

↗

Measure impact

Estimate emissions from fuel or electricity use and make the assumptions visible.

Translate an emissions-reduction ambition into a clear emissions budget.
